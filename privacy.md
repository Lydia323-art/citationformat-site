## Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how this APA citation generator handles information when you use the site.

## Information You Enter

You may enter a DOI or citation details such as author names, article title, journal name, year, volume, issue, pages, DOI, or URL.

In the MVP version, this site does not upload PDF files, store PDF files, create user accounts, or save bibliography projects.

## DOI Lookup

When you use DOI lookup, the DOI may be sent to Crossref to retrieve citation metadata such as title, authors, journal, and publication date.

## Analytics

The site may collect basic usage analytics, such as page views, citation generation events, copy button events, and error types. These analytics help improve the tool.

Analytics configuration is pending. If analytics or advertising services are enabled, this policy and the Cookie Policy should be updated before launch.

## Hosting and Operations

The site may be hosted on Cloudflare Pages and may use Cloudflare Workers for DOI lookup. Hosting providers may process operational logs needed for security, reliability, and abuse prevention.

## What We Do Not Do in the MVP

- We do not upload PDF files.
- We do not store PDF files.
- We do not save your bibliography.
- We do not create accounts.
- We do not process payments.
