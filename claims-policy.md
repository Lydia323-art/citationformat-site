## Approved Claims

- Free APA 7 citation generator.
- Generate APA citations from a DOI or article details.
- Copy reference and in-text citations.
- Use manual entry when DOI lookup is unavailable.
- No PDF upload in the MVP.
- No account required in the MVP.

## Do Not Claim

- Official APA tool.
- Official ScienceDirect tool.
- 100% accurate citations.
- Guaranteed acceptance by any instructor or institution.
- Unlimited usage.
- Endorsed by Crossref, MyBib, Scribbr, QuillBot, Grammarly, APA, or ScienceDirect.
- PDF upload or PDF parsing in the MVP.
