## Terms of Use

By using this site, you agree to use it as an independent citation formatting tool.

## Citation Accuracy

The tool is designed to help format APA 7 citations, but citation requirements may vary by instructor, institution, publisher, or assignment. Review generated citations before submitting your work.

## No Official Affiliation

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by APA, ScienceDirect, Crossref, MyBib, Scribbr, QuillBot, Grammarly, or any other third-party citation platform or publisher mentioned on the site.

## DOI Metadata

DOI lookup may use third-party metadata sources such as Crossref. Metadata may be incomplete or unavailable. If DOI lookup fails or returns partial information, use the manual form to review and complete your citation.

## Acceptable Use

Do not use the site for automated scraping, abusive requests, attempts to disrupt the service, or unlawful activity. Automated or excessive requests may be limited.

## Payments

The MVP version does not offer paid plans, subscriptions, checkout, or refunds.
